**A larger character redesign is not warranted by this matrix.** It neither demonstrates a recurring stylistic improvement nor proves that the existing family lacks character. X/Y are versions of the same family, with limited text coverage and no competitor condition; this is particularly weak evidence for judging absolute distinctiveness. The coherent Cyrillic and short-text assessments support keeping the present moderate compact direction. A stronger personality could be a separate creative brief, but should not be presented as fixing an observed recognition failure.

Acceptance: inspect randomized Il1 identifiers at 12/14/16/18px, weights 400/500/700, explicit opsz9/14/16 and production automatic sizing, on target browser/native renderers; inspect spacing/clipping and conduct blinded human transcription. A 48px visual preference does not establish fewer small-UI errors.
- **O/0:** agree with the type report's use-case limitation. Our I preference supplies no evidence that O/0 improved. If identifiers are in scope, use an optional differentiated-zero feature or another explicit identifier treatment, without requiring a wholesale prose redesign. Acceptance: show that the chosen treatment adds a cue beyond width in random O/0 tokens at 14/18px, then measure human identification errors against the untreated version under a predeclared criterion.
- **Cyrillic:** agree that the reports identify no reproducible construction failure. Flat-top Л/Д are not a sufficient defect argument. Acceptance for broader character claims: native-reader review of longer Russian passages with recurring л/д/п/и/ш/щ and б/в/ь/ъ, at intended sizes, with shuffled repeated comparisons.
- **U+2022:** reject `CYR-01` as a defect in the contracted inventory. Coverage evidence reports exactly 189 expected and actual codepoints, no missing/extra, and bullet outside that set. The observed missing bullet remains a valid integration or future coverage issue. Acceptance: explicitly document that scope and verify configured fallback where product text uses a bullet; if scope is expanded, require a nonzero shaped bullet and appropriate appearance across instances.
- **Reading claims:** agree across reports that sustained reading is unestablished. Acceptance: counterbalanced human RU/EN sessions of 10–15 minutes against a named reference at matched apparent size, with comprehension, errors and discomfort measured. Predeclare success criteria; model agreement cannot replace participants.

Application verdicts remain: UI conditional; headings provisional visual pass; body conditional based on short proofs; sustained reading not established. No larger redesign acceptance gate follows from this evidence. If recurring character is commissioned, require paragraph-level blind comparisons excluding isolated diagnostic letters, repeat/shuffle conditions, and actual readers before claiming recognition gains.

Limitations: discussion combines agent visual reports and supplied coverage evidence, not independent human data; no additional rendered proof was created; the scope of each renderer and sample still applies. Cross-review agreement is not a quantified recognition result.
